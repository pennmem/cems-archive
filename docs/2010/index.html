April 27 - April 29, 2010, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

The 2010 Context and Episodic Memory Symposium (CEMS) was held in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, on the days of April 28th and April 29th. The symposium is designed to be a forum for the exchange of ideas among colleagues working on theoretical and empirical approaches to the study of context and episodic memory, broadly construed.

Hotel

The symposium was held at the Rittenhouse Hotel in Philadelphia.

Registration

The registration fee of $250 includes breakfast, refreshments, and lunches. Registration is closed.

Posters

The Symposium will include a poster session. Posters should be no larger than 40" by 60".
